Our Purpose

Mission

What We Do Today

To dismantle digital, physical, and social barriers in Bhutan by providing expert accessibility audits, localizing speech and language technologies, and empowering organizations with WCAG-compliant tools and inclusive workplace training.

Vision

Where We Are Heading

A fully inclusive Bhutanese society where individuals of all abilities—regardless of visual, auditory, physical, or cognitive challenges—have equal access to technology, education, employment, and public life.

About Our Consultancy

Bhutan Accessibility and Inclusion Consultancy (BAIC) is a specialized initiative dedicated to ensuring every Bhutanese citizen has equal access to technology, infrastructure, and public resources.

Digital Accessibility

Auditing and engineering web portals, mobile apps, and Dzongkha language technology to support screen reader users and assistive devices.

Assistive Technologies

Exploring and distributing local mobility aids, smart white canes, and assistive learning tools for the blind and visually impaired community.

Policy & Advocacy

Guiding institutions toward international WCAG compliance, inclusive employment frameworks, and accessible educational infrastructure.

Core Services

1. Web & Mobile Accessibility Audits

Thorough manual and automated testing against WCAG 2.1 / 2.2 AA standards using NVDA, TalkBack, and keyboard navigation audits.

2. Dzongkha Language & Speech Tech Localization

Building accessible open-source language models, speech technology, and localization projects to ensure full native language digital inclusion.

3. Disability Awareness & Workplace Training

Conducting workshops for organizations, schools, and web developers on inclusive design, accessible content creation, and workplace accommodations.
