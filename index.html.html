Strategic Guidance

Our Mission

To eliminate physical, digital, and attitudinal barriers across Bhutan by providing expert accessibility auditing, policy advocacy, community training, and cost-effective assistive technology deployment in line with the Gross National Happiness framework.

Our Vision

An inclusive Bhutanese society where individuals of all abilities enjoy equal rights, dignified independence, seamless participation in community life, and unhindered access to public infrastructure and digital services.

About BAIC

The Bhutan Accessibility and Inclusion Consultancy (BAIC) is a specialized consultancy firm established to support government bodies, civil society organizations, educational institutions, and international partners in building inclusive environments for persons with disabilities in Bhutan.

Rooted in national values of social harmony and compassion, BAIC bridges international accessibility standards (such as WCAG 2.2 and Universal Design Principles) with localized solution-building tailored to Bhutan's unique topography and infrastructure.
